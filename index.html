FAQ.

What does EZ Check actually read?

Real credit, income and debt to income, pulled on a soft inquiry the moment a lead comes in. You work from the actual numbers instead of what someone typed into a form.

Does it affect my customer's credit score?

No. Pre-qualification is a soft pull and does not affect their credit score. A hard inquiry only happens later at full application, and only with the customer's authorization.

How fast are results?

Seconds. The lead is routed while they're still warm.

Who decides where each lead goes?

You do. You set the criteria. Leads above it go to your sales calendar, leads below it go to a lower-ticket offer, and you can send the rest into any funnel you choose.

Do I need a new website or CRM?

No. The smart form drops into your existing funnel. On the demo we map it onto the way you capture and work leads today.

Is EZ Check a lender?

No. EZ Check pre-qualifies leads. Financing is provided by independent third-party lenders and is subject to their approval.
