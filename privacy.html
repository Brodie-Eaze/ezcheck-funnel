Privacy Policy.

Last updated June 18, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy describes how AI Funding Solutions collects, uses, discloses, and protects information, and the choices and rights you have. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

1Introduction and scope

AI Funding Solutions (“AI Funding Solutions,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a software company organized as a Wyoming limited liability company. We provide software that businesses install into their existing sales and marketing funnels to capture leads, pre-qualify them on real financial data, route them, and facilitate financing through third-party lenders at the point of sale.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information in connection with our websites (including ai-funding-solutions-production.up.railway.app and any successor or related domains), our software, applications, application programming interfaces, embedded forms, and any related services, content, and communications (collectively, the “Services”). It applies to information about prospective and current business customers and their personnel, website visitors, and consumers who interact with our pre-qualification tools through a business that uses our Services.

We are not a lender, broker-dealer, credit repair organization, or investment, financial, legal, or tax adviser, and we do not make credit decisions. We provide software and facilitate introductions to third-party lenders. Financing is offered, underwritten, approved, and funded solely by third-party lenders under their own terms and privacy practices.

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, do not access or use the Services. This Privacy Policy is incorporated into and forms part of our Terms of Service.

2Our role: controller and processor

For information we collect about our own website visitors, prospective customers, and account holders, and for our own marketing, security, analytics, and product-improvement purposes, we act as a controller (or “business” under U.S. state privacy laws).

When a business installs our software and uses it to collect or process information about its own leads and consumers, that business is the controller of that information and we act as a service provider or processor that handles the information on the business’s behalf and under our agreement with that business. If you are a consumer who submitted information through a business that uses our Services, please direct privacy requests to that business in the first instance; we will support that business in responding as required by law.

5Soft-pull pre-qualification and the FCRA

Our pre-qualification tools may facilitate a “soft” credit inquiry (soft pull) through a consumer reporting agency or data provider, performed with the applicable consumer’s consent and for a permissible purpose under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), 15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq. A soft pull is used to estimate eligibility and does not affect the consumer’s credit score.

A “hard” credit inquiry, which may affect a credit score, occurs only at the lender’s full application stage and only with the consumer’s separate authorization to the lender. We are not a consumer reporting agency, we do not furnish or resell consumer reports, and we do not make credit or adverse-action decisions. Credit decisions, including any adverse-action notices required under the FCRA and the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA), are the responsibility of the lender or other decisioning party.

The Services use automated tools, models, and software agents to capture, score, pre-qualify, and route leads and to surface potential financing options. Our models are designed not to use protected-class characteristics as inputs. Automated outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be relied upon as financial, legal, or eligibility advice. Where required by applicable law, you may have the right to request information about, or to object to, certain automated processing; see Section 14 and contact us at admin@amala.agency.
